How I keep software reliable.

Good implementation is only part of it. I also plan for exceptions, recovery, handover, and the people responsible after launch.

  1. Map the process

    Identify who uses it, where the data comes from, which decisions matter, and where handoffs break down.

  2. Plan for failures

    Decide how validation, duplicate checks, logging, recovery, and ownership should work before implementation.

  3. Build it as one system

    Treat the interface, data, background jobs, APIs, and documentation as one piece of work.

  4. Stay involved after launch

    Support production use, investigate issues, and extend the software as the business changes.
