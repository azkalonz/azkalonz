# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, operations leaders, founders, and technical decision-makers evaluating whether Mark Judaya can understand and deliver custom software, automation, integration, consulting, or support work.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio helps a potential client understand Mark's value quickly, see substantial evidence of his work, and start a direct project conversation.

## Positioning

Mark turns complicated operational workflows into dependable, maintainable systems. He works across the full problem: understanding the process, building the application, connecting surrounding platforms, protecting reliability, and improving the system after launch.

## Operating Context

The work centers on internal applications, product and customer data, order workflows, CRM and ERP systems, marketplace integrations, automation, migrations, and production support. Projects may involve React, TypeScript, Laravel, PHP, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Zoho, Salesforce, Mirakl, REST APIs, n8n, CSV pipelines, and webhooks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the routes, case-study URLs, contact and booking paths, analytics, prerendering, SEO semantics, and responsive web behavior.
- The primary conversion is starting a project conversation; booking a call is a secondary route.
- AI integration is one capability, not the defining service.
- Both light and dark themes are required, must be independently art-directed, and must respect system preference by default without a flash.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, outcomes, metrics, technologies, certifications, employment history, or awards.

## Brand Commitments

- Present the portfolio under the BuiltByMark.dev name while keeping Mark Judaya's personal identity, Cebu/Philippines location, direct professional links, and factual project content available where relevant.
- Present Mark as an experienced technical partner for operational systems rather than a generic freelancer or a visual-experience developer.
- Lead with business problems and proof. Technology remains useful supporting evidence.
- The experience should feel calm, precise, authored, technically credible, and human.

## Evidence on Hand

- Four service definitions and practical delivery language in `src/data/site.ts`.
- Four real project records and supporting case-study detail in `src/data/projects.ts` and `public/project-details/`.
- Urban Road PIM product screenshots in `public/project-screens/urban-road-pim/`.
- Verified proof includes more than 2 million migrated records, 673 governed field mappings, eight enabled export destinations, a six-step import process, duplicate protection, validation, scheduled syncing, incident logging, resumable processing, audit history, and production support.
- Existing contact, booking, GitHub, LinkedIn, Fiverr, analytics, sitemap, robots, metadata, portrait, and identity assets.
- No testimonials, client-logo collection, awards, performance benchmarks, or AI-focused case study is supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the operational outcome before the implementation detail.
2. Prove capability through real workflows, safeguards, and case-study evidence.
3. Make access to Mark feel direct and low-friction.
4. Keep technical depth available without technical theater.
5. Prefer maintainable, accessible, performant delivery over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic headings and landmarks, useful image alternatives, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, comfortable reading measures, accessible controls, and responsive layouts without hiding project evidence.
