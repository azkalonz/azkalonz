# Mark Judaya

I’m a full-stack developer and IT solutions specialist based in Cebu, Philippines. I build dependable applications, integrations, and automation around the way a business actually works.

My work covers the full problem: understanding the process, shaping a practical solution, building the interface and supporting services, connecting surrounding platforms, and keeping the system useful after launch.

## What I build

- Web and mobile applications, internal tools, portals, and dashboards.
- CRM, ERP, marketplace, and third-party integrations.
- Workflow automation, data migration, and synchronisation.
- Technical discovery, application improvements, and production support.
- Focused AI-assisted workflows grounded in operational data.

## Selected work

- [Urban Road PIM](https://builtbymark.dev/projects/urban-road-pim) — governed product data, validation, review, and channel-ready exports.
- [Salesforce to Zoho CRM migration](https://builtbymark.dev/projects/salesforce-to-zoho-crm-migration) — relationship-preserving migration with validation and traceability.
- [Mirakl and Zoho Inventory integration](https://builtbymark.dev/projects/mirakl-zoho-inventory-integration) — connected order, shipment, document, and incident workflows.
- [Zoho Inventory ERP integration](https://builtbymark.dev/projects/zoho-inventory-erp-integration) — automated fulfilment, production, shipping, and invoicing data.

## How I work

1. Clarify the goal, users, constraints, handoffs, and failure cases.
2. Design for validation, recovery, auditability, and ownership.
3. Build maintainable systems that can change without starting again.

I commonly work with React, TypeScript, Laravel, PHP, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, REST APIs, webhooks, n8n, Zoho, Salesforce, Mirakl, and ERP workflows.
